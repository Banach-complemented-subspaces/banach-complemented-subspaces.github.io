# arXiv logo artwork

`arxiv-logo.jpg` is an unchanged local copy of the official arXiv primary logo:

- Asset: https://info.arxiv.org/brand/images/brand-logo-primary.jpg
- Official logo page: https://info.arxiv.org/brand/logos.html
- Brand-use guidance: https://info.arxiv.org/brand/brand-guidelines.html

Retrieved 6 September 2026 for this private local companion. The original colours, proportions and built-in clear space are preserved. The logo is served locally, with no remote image requests. arXiv owns its name and logo; this companion does not claim affiliation or endorsement. The logo will link to the manuscript's arXiv entry once its public URL is supplied.
